What's moving, and why

  • Staggered entrance (~0.02s apart) — nav, then heading, KPIs, cards. Runs once per page load, never on re-render.
  • Numbers count up — draws the eye to the figure that matters. Capped at ~0.9s.
  • Chart draws itself — the line traces, then the fill fades in behind it.
  • Skeletons shaped like the content — click Simulate data fetch. No spinner, no layout shift when data lands.
  • Hover — cards lift 3px, rows tint, buttons lift; all ~0.2s.
  • Press — buttons scale to 0.97 so a click feels physical.
  • Slide-over — panel eases in over a blurred scrim; Esc or click-away closes it.
  • Optimistic confirmation — hit Approve: the pill flips to Approved instantly and a toast rises. The screen never blocks you.
  • Reduced motion — toggle it: every animation collapses to ~0. Non-negotiable for accessibility.
